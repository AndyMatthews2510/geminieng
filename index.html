From Lab Report to Journal Article: The Rhetorical Shift

Dimension Coursework / Lab Report Engineering Journal Article (IEEE, ASME, Elsevier)
Audience Course instructor assessing procedural compliance. International researchers and practitioners seeking new knowledge.
Narrative Arc Chronological diary (*"First we did X, then measured Y"*). Rhetorical problem-solution argument proving novelty.
Handling Errors Often concealed or blamed on vague "human error." Characterized using error propagation, bounds, and physical mechanism.

IMRaD Section Guidelines

  • Introduction (CARS Model): (1) Establish territory $\rightarrow$ (2) Establish niche (identify the gap) $\rightarrow$ (3) Occupy niche with explicit contributions.
  • Methodology: Strictly procedural and reproducible. State equipment models, uncertainty tolerances, calibration states, and boundary conditions.
  • Results vs. Discussion: Results state factual observations and data trends. Discussion interprets mechanisms, benchmarks against literature, and confronts limitations.
